# 🌍 Environmental Impact Analysis Dashboard (Tableau)

## 📁 Project Overview
This project presents an interactive *Environmental Impact Dashboard, designed using **Tableau*. 
The dashboard evaluates key global environmental and sustainability indicators, tracking total carbon emissions, water consumption, energy consumption trends over time, recycling efficiency across industries and regions, regulatory compliance distributions, and the correlation between emissions and recycling rates.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Highlights
The dashboard includes the following key visualizations:

1. *Sustainability KPI Scorecards*
   - *Total Carbon Emissions:* 16.2M kg
   - *Average Recycling Rate:* 72.02%
   - *Total Energy Usage:* 37.5M kWh
   - *Total Water Consumption:* 49.9M Liters

2. *Total Water Consumption by Region*
   - Bar chart illustrating regional water usage led by *North America (7.3M L), **Asia (7.2M L), **Middle East (7.2M L), **South America (7.1M L), **Africa (7.1M L), **Europe (7.0M L), and **Australia (7.0M L)*.

3. *Trends of Energy Usage Over Time (2020–2025)*
   - Line chart tracking global energy demand trajectory, showing an initial rise from 2021 (9.05M) to a massive peak in 2022 (21.01M), followed by a steep post-2022 stabilization around ~3.5M–3.8M.

4. *Recycling Rate by Region and Industry*
   - Heatmap matrix comparing recycling percentages across sectors (Automotive, Construction, Energy, Food Processing, Mining, Technology, Textiles) against all geographic regions.

5. *Distribution of Compliance Levels*
   - Donut chart depicting regulatory environmental compliance evenly distributed across *High (33.5%), **Medium (33.4%), and **Low (33.1%)*.

6. *Relationship Between Carbon Emission and Recycling Rate*
   - Scatter plot examining emissions (kg) against recycling percentages to assess operational sustainability tradeoffs.

---

## 💡 Key Insights
The dashboard reveals the following key takeaways:

1. *Water Consumption Parity:* Regional water consumption is heavily distributed across all operating markets, with *North America and Asia* demonstrating the highest total resource draw (~7.2M–7.3M Liters each).
2. *Post-2022 Energy De-escalation:* After a peak of *21M in 2022*, energy usage dropped sharply by over 80% to maintain stable levels (~3.5M–3.8M) through 2024 and 2025.
3. *Balanced Regulatory Compliance:* Compliance records show an even 1/3 split across High, Medium, and Low tiers, indicating significant opportunity to elevate bottom-tier facilities to higher environmental standards.
4. *Stable Sector Recycling:* Across manufacturing sectors (Textiles, Automotive, Technology), baseline recycling rates consistently hover around the *70%–74%* threshold across most geographic operating hubs.

## 🎯 Final Conclusion & Recommendations
* *Upgrade Low-Compliance Segments:* With 33.1% of audited facilities operating at "Low" compliance, institute automated audit schedules and standardized green building/production protocols to transition them into the High-compliance bracket.
* *Target Water-Intensive Regions:* Implement water recycling and closed-loop cooling systems in North American and Asian production hubs to curb high regional consumption footprints.
* *Maintain Energy Optimization Strategies:* Standardize the post-2022 operational efficiencies that successfully curtailed peak energy usage down to current low baseline thresholds.
